>>===== MODE =====>>
citation
<<===== MODE =====<<


>>===== DESCRIPTION =====>>
Implicit year-suffix disambiguation must place the suffix immediately after the
rendered year date-part, even when another date-part follows it.
<<===== DESCRIPTION =====<<


>>===== RESULT =====>>
Smith 1980a May; Smith 1980b May
<<===== RESULT =====<<
