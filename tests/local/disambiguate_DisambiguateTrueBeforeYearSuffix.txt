citation

>>===== DESCRIPTION =====>>
Conditional citation text must be tried before year-suffix disambiguation. Two items
with the same author and year are distinguished by their titles, so no suffixes
should be assigned.
<<===== DESCRIPTION =====<<


>>===== RESULT =====>>
